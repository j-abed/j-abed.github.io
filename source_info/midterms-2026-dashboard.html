Data

Results. Decision Desk HQ race pages, read on Nov 4, 2026 (state races early morning, House around midday ET). A race is "called" only when DDHQ has made a call; everything else is shown as a lead. Percentages reported as ">95%" are stored as 95.

Baselines. 2024 presidential results by state (FEC) and by congressional district (Daily Kos Elections); prior results for the same seat from Wikipedia. States that redrew House maps for 2026 use presidential figures re-aggregated to the new lines where available.

Polling. Final published averages (RealClearPolling, Silver Bulletin, Race to the WH, via Wikipedia aggregation tables). Races without an established aggregate are excluded from polling-error statistics.

Ratings and forecasts. Final Cook Political Report, Sabato's Crystal Ball and Inside Elections ratings (Oct 29–Nov 2), DDHQ's own House categories, and published seat projections from Silver Bulletin and DDHQ.

Demographics. ACS 2018–22 (education, income) and 2020 Census (race, urbanization) by state and by 118th-Congress district; FiveThirtyEight urbanization index.

Measures

Margin. Two-party: (D − R)/(D + R) × 100, so third-party and top-two same-party contests don't distort swing.

Swing. 2026 margin minus the baseline margin. "Uniform swing" is the median across races; the local residual is each race's swing minus that median.

Polling error. Actual margin minus final-average margin; positive means polls understated Democrats. Confidence intervals are percentile bootstraps of the mean.

Calibration. Ratings mapped to conventional implied probabilities (Solid .98, Likely .85, Lean .70, Toss-up .50). The Brier score compares these with outcomes; climatology is the score from predicting the overall D win rate for every race.

Caveats. Counts are preliminary and will move in late-counting states (CA, AZ, WA, OR, NV, AK). Regression coefficients describe association, not cause; district-level demographic models exclude the nine states that redrew their maps.
